Exam Flow and Scoring

Dedicated speaking runtime with strict IELTS behavior, hidden telemetry, and post-session grading

Session Flow

1. Setup

User chooses full test or a single part, checks mic, and starts an authenticated session.

2. Live Examiner

Gemini Live acts only as the examiner. English only. No score, no hints, no praise during the exam.

3. Candidate Turn

When examiner audio ends, VAD opens the candidate turn and records raw speech + timestamps.

4. Final Grading

After session close, backend runs a separate evaluator over transcript, turn metadata, and stored audio.

Strict but Smart Behavior
Default Exam Rules

Examiner controls order and pacing.

User cannot steer the conversation away.

Examiner asks follow-ups only inside IELTS logic.

Part 2 includes one-minute prep and timed long turn.

Part 3 stays linked to the Part 2 theme.

Allowed Smart Repairs

Repeat the question once.

Mic or audio clarification.

Very short off-topic drift warning.

Non-English reminder to answer in English.

Integrity Policy

Minor Violation

  • Single off-topic answer
  • Short non-English reply
  • Candidate asks examiner to help
  • One warning, then continue if corrected

Major Violation

  • Abuse, hate, sexual content, threats
  • Prompt-injection or role-break attempts
  • Repeated refusal to answer in English
  • Automatic stop and penalized band
Scoring Output
Final evaluation stored in backend

Band scores for Fluency and Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy, and Pronunciation.

Exact raw phrases stay unfixed in evidence snippets when candidate says words incorrectly.

Feedback includes strengths, critical weaknesses, pronunciation issues, useful corrections, and deep advice.

Live telemetry can inform fluency and pronunciation, but user sees feedback only after grading finishes.
