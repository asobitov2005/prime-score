Data Model

speaking_tests

Published mock sets, format full/part, estimated minutes, source window, status.

speaking_topics

Part 1 topics, Part 2 cue cards, Part 3 follow-up clusters, seed bank 20+ each.

speaking_sessions

User run, live provider ids, state machine, warning count, termination reason, timing.

speaking_turns

Examiner/candidate turns, raw transcript, no cleanup of mispronounced words, timestamps.

speaking_audio_assets

Chunk manifests, final merged audio, diarization metadata, per-part recordings.

speaking_evaluations

Criterion scores, strengths, issues, exact problematic phrases, advice, deep feedback.
